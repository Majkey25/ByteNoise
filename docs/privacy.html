Privacy and use

Updated 5 October 2026. ByteNoise is maintained by Matěj Teplý (Majkey). Contact: majkeylab@gmail.com.

Your text stays on your device

The browser app converts text and selected files in this tab. It does not upload their contents, use analytics, create accounts, or set cookies or local storage. Clear removes the displayed text. Closing the tab ends the app session; your browser may offer session restoration. Delete saved files or clipboard copies separately.

The Python desktop app also performs conversion locally. This website notice describes the hosted browser version.

Hosting and contact

GitHub Pages serves this website and processes technical requests, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. GitHub determines its hosting-data retention and international transfer safeguards in its privacy statement. The maintainer does not receive your conversion text from GitHub.

If you email the maintainer, your address and message are used to answer and document your request, based on the legitimate interest in providing support. Correspondence is kept only while needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or confidential text.

Your choices and rights

For personal information held by the maintainer, use the contact above to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing. Portability applies where the law provides it. You can also contact the Czech data protection authority or your local authority. For GitHub's hosting records, use the contacts in GitHub's privacy statement.

Free tool and limits

There are no purchases, subscriptions, or fees in ByteNoise, so there is no in-app refund process. ByteNoise is reversible obfuscation, not secure encryption. Do not use it to protect passwords, keys, or private data. The MIT license covers the source. Nothing here limits mandatory legal rights.
